# Промпты для генерации изображений

Все изображения должны быть реалистичными, без текста, без чужих логотипов, в теплой карамельно-коричневой палитре.

---

## 1. Профессиональный чистый автосервис

> Realistic photograph of a professional auto repair shop interior. Clean, well-lit workshop with modern diagnostic equipment, hydraulic lifts, and organized tool cabinets. Warm caramel and brown color accents in the interior design. No people. No text or logos visible. Professional automotive workshop atmosphere. 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution.

---

## 2. Легковой автомобиль на диагностике

> Realistic photograph of a passenger car (sedan) on a lift or diagnostic station inside a clean auto repair shop. A diagnostic tablet or laptop connected to the vehicle's OBD port is visible. Professional workshop lighting with warm brown and caramel tones. No visible license plates, no text, no logos. Focus on the vehicle and diagnostic equipment. 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution.

---

## 3. Грузовой автомобиль или прицеп в ремонтной зоне

> Realistic photograph of a large truck or trailer inside a spacious commercial vehicle repair bay. High ceiling workshop with heavy-duty lifts and industrial lighting. Warm brown and caramel color accents. Professional setting. No visible license plates, no text, no logos. Focus on the scale of the vehicle and workshop. 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution.

---

## 4. Механик с диагностическим оборудованием

> Realistic photograph of a professional auto mechanic (face not clearly visible) using modern diagnostic equipment while inspecting a vehicle. The mechanic wears clean professional workwear in neutral dark colors. Clean workshop background with warm caramel and brown tones. No text or logos visible. Professional, trustworthy atmosphere. 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution.

---

## 5. Крупный план деталей и инструментов

> Realistic close-up photograph of automotive repair tools and quality spare parts neatly arranged on a clean workbench. Professional-grade wrenches, sockets, diagnostic tools, and automotive components. Warm lighting with brown and caramel tones in the background. Shallow depth of field. No text or logos visible. 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution.
